# Heaven & Nature design principles

## Direction

Organic refined, not black-and-gold luxury. Warm paper, cedar brown, botanical green, transparent glass and restrained peach are the core materials. The gold monogram belongs as a real brand asset, not as generic decorative filler.

## Non-negotiables

- No purple gradients, glassmorphism panels, default SaaS cards or pill-button patterns.
- Use Instrument Serif for display, DM Sans for readable body copy and DM Mono only for quiet utility labels.
- Let real perfume photography replace illustration, never sit beside it as fake product proof.
- Motion is limited to opacity and transform, honours reduced-motion preferences and cannot obstruct reading.
- Product copy must stay specific and owner-confirmed. No invented scent, performance, safety or availability claims.

## Memorable element

The clear-glass bottle and warm wooden cap are the visual anchor. The page uses the contrast between an airy editorial surface and a physical-looking object, rather than a standard product-grid storefront.
